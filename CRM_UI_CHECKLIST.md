# CRM UX Checklist (Best-of Review)

Use this while reviewing Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, Zoho, and Pipedrive.

## Must-Have Daily Workflow
- Fast "what should I do now" queue at top.
- One-click actions: call, text, schedule, quote follow-up.
- Clear ownership on every lead and UPS item.
- Due-date and overdue visibility without opening detail pages.

## Pipeline Experience
- Stage board with simple stage-change controls.
- Card-level context: customer, store, owner, next action, due date.
- Minimal clicks to move from New -> Contacted -> Appointment -> Quoted -> Won/Lost.

## UPS / Showroom Operations
- Store selector first.
- Rep self check-in to UPS line.
- Start customer with type: Regular Up or B-Back.
- Complete customer and auto-reposition rep:
  - Regular Up -> move to end of line.
  - B-Back -> move to front of line.
- Visual "AVAILABLE" vs "WORKING" status per rep.

## Contact 360
- Single panel for phone, notes, last message, next action, and stage.
- Edit-in-place fields (no modal dependency).
- Quick template insert for common replies.

## Management / Team Controls
- Scope toggle: My Leads vs Team Leads.
- Per-store filtering and accountability views.
- Activity snapshot (today touched, overdue, wins).

## Quality Bar
- Enter key submits common actions.
- Mobile-friendly spacing and tap targets.
- No dead-end pages; every view should have a next action.
